# Neuroception Calibration: Your Body's Radar

Before you can change how you feel, you must accurately detect it. Stephen W. Porges (2025) defines **Neuroception** as our nervous system's subconscious radar for detecting safety, danger, or life-threat.

When you feel unexpectedly overwhelmed, your neuroception has likely misidentified a neutral cue (like an email) as a life-threat.

### Step 1: Baseline Readout (Observe)
Take 30 seconds to observe your physical state. Don't judge it. Just locate the tension.

### Step 2: Somatic Intervention (Intervene)
We will use Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia (RSA) to manually apply the "vagal brake" to your heart rate.

### Step 3: Neuro-Mechanistic Synthesis (Integrate)
Notice the shift? By extending your exhalations, you just signaled to your vagus nerve that you are in a safe environment, forcing your heart rate to decouple from the sympathetic stress response.
