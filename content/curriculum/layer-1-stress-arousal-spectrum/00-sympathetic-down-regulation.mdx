# Sympathetic Down-Regulation: The Physiological Sigh

Allostatic load is the "wear and tear" on your body from chronic stress. To rapidly dump accumulated allostatic load, we bypass the cognitive brain entirely.

### Step 1: Baseline Readout (Observe)
Where is your respiration rate right now? Shallow chest breathing or deep diaphragmatic breathing?

<SomaticCheckIn />

### Step 2: Somatic Intervention (Intervene)
Perform the Physiological Sigh: Two quick inhales through the nose, followed by one long exhale through the mouth.

<PhysiologicalSighTool duration="90s" />

### Step 3: Neuro-Mechanistic Synthesis (Integrate)
The double-inhale pops open collapsed alveoli in the lungs, allowing off-gassing of carbon dioxide. The long exhale dramatically increases vagal tone. This is the fastest known autonomic down-regulator.
