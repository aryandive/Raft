# Prefrontal-Amygdala Decoupling: Cognitive Override

When the amygdala detects a threat (even an emotional one), it can hijack the prefrontal cortex, reducing your ability to think logically. We must practice intentionally re-engaging executive function during stress.

### Step 1: Baseline Readout
Think of a moderately stressful upcoming event. Notice the immediate physiological shift in your chest or stomach.

<SomaticCheckIn />

### Step 2: Somatic Intervention
We will use a dual-task protocol: perform deep box breathing while simultaneously completing a complex cognitive visualization task (e.g., counting backwards from 100 by 7s).

<DualTaskProtocol duration="120s" />

### Step 3: Neuro-Mechanistic Synthesis
By forcing the prefrontal cortex to perform complex math while the body is in a state of induced regulation (via breathing), you actively strengthen the neural pathways that allow top-down suppression of the amygdala's alarm bells.

### Step 4: Encrypted Synthesis
Reflect on how the dual task affected the intensity of your initial stressful thought.

<VaultReflection prompt="Did the cognitive load of counting disrupt the physical feeling of stress?" />
