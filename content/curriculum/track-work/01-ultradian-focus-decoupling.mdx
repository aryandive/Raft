# Post-Work Psychological Detachment: The Cognitive Shutdown

Modern knowledge work creates an insidious autonomic pathology known as **"Allostatic Spillover"**—the inability of the prefrontal cortex to disengage from workplace tasks after hours. 

Dr. Sabine Sonnentag's seminal research (2018) shows that individuals who fail to mentally detach during non-work hours experience accelerated emotional exhaustion, reduced executive function the next morning, and chronic sympathetic elevation.
