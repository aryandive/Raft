# Visceral Signal Decoding: Translating the Body

Interoception is the perception of sensations from inside the body (heartbeat, respiration, satiety). High interoceptive accuracy is strongly correlated with emotional resilience.

### Step 1: Baseline Readout
Close your eyes and attempt to count your heartbeats for 30 seconds without taking your pulse.

<SomaticCheckIn />

### Step 2: Somatic Intervention
We will practice "Body Mapping." Focus on three distinct zones: your jaw, your stomach, and your hands. Intentionally tense and then fully release each zone.

<GroundingTool type="progressive_relaxation" duration="180s" />

### Step 3: Neuro-Mechanistic Synthesis
By consciously tracking visceral states, you strengthen the insular cortex, which acts as the brain's "hub" for integrating physical sensations with emotional awareness. This prevents the brain from making faulty "threat" predictions.

### Step 4: Encrypted Synthesis
Record how accurately you felt your heartbeat, and any specific tension zones you identified.
